# Frame It

Add clean borders to your photos and resize them for Instagram, right in your browser.

### 👉 [Open Frame It](https://xarvulth.github.io/frame-it/)

## Features

- **Size presets**: Instagram post (4:5, 1080×1350), Square (1:1, 1080×1080), Story (9:16, 1080×1920) and Landscape (1.91:1, 1080×566)
- **Border width** as a percentage of the image width, from 0% to 15% in 0.5% steps
- **Border colors**: white, warm white, black, or any custom color
- **Photo placement**: *Show whole photo* (fit) or *Fill and crop* (fill)
- **Original ratio** (1080 wide), **Original, large** (2048 px long edge) and **Full size** (no resizing, all detail)
- **Export** as JPEG (92% quality, good for Instagram) or lossless PNG
